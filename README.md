# Silicon M.A Group Sales Analytics – Tableau

## Project Overview

The **Silicon M.A Group Sales Analytics** project is an interactive Tableau dashboard developed to examine the company's sales performance for 2014.

The dashboard provides an overview of revenue generation, product category performance, customer activity, salesperson contributions, regional distribution, and monthly sales trends.

It brings key sales indicators together in a single interactive reporting environment to support business performance monitoring and analysis.

## Project Objectives

The analysis focuses on:

- Evaluating overall revenue performance.
- Examining revenue contribution by product category.
- Comparing sales performance across regions and states.
- Identifying high-performing salespeople.
- Analyzing customer activity by quantity.
- Monitoring average unit price and shipping fees.
- Exploring monthly and regional revenue trends.
- Comparing sales performance across cities.

## Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard highlights four major metrics:

- **Total Revenue:** $435,066
- **Average Unit Price:** $21.43
- **Average Quantity:** 56.26
- **Total Shipping Fee:** $43,663

## Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Revenue by Category

Examines revenue generated across five product categories:

- Coffee
- Mozzarella
- Beer
- Curry Sauce
- Crab Meat

Coffee recorded the highest revenue among the displayed categories.

### 2. Salesperson Performance

Evaluates individual salesperson contributions to revenue, helping identify differences in sales performance.

### 3. Regional Performance

Examines revenue distribution across the East, West, North, and South regions.

### 4. State and City Analysis

Uses geographic and comparative visualizations to examine revenue distribution across different states and cities.

New York recorded the highest city revenue among the locations displayed.

### 5. Regional Revenue Trends

The monthly revenue trend visualization provides a view of changes in regional sales performance throughout 2014.

### 6. Customer Analysis

Examines customer activity using quantity-based metrics to compare contributions across selected companies.

## Interactive Features

The dashboard includes filters for:

- Region
- Salesperson
- Customer Name
- City

These filters allow users to explore different business segments and examine sales performance from multiple perspectives.
